Epoch shows the current moment across a wide range of time systems —
updating every second, all in one place.

<b>What Epoch gives you</b>
- Civil: date, time, day second, day percent — for any timezone
- Technical: Unix time, International Atomic Time (TAI), GPS time
- Astronomical: Greenwich and Local Mean Sidereal Time (GMST, LMST), Julian Date variants
- Curiosities: Swatch Internet Time, seven-segment and binary clocks, Doomsday Clock
Copy any value to clipboard with one tap.

<b>Configurable</b>
- Build your own Watchlist tabs — combine any value from any category, in any order.
- Set custom labels, section headers, and dividers.
- Reorder by drag-and-drop, swipe to remove.
- Customize date and time formats, configure timezone information.
- Optionally pin timezones to always show standard or summer time.
- 7-day advance notice of clock changes, with upcoming transitions shown in the value's info dialog.
- Export, import, and reset all preferences.

<b>Focus mode</b>
- Double-tap any value for a full-screen, always-on display — ideal as a bedside clock.
- Adjustable brightness (Android) and text color.
- Toggle details with a long press.

<b>Designed for night use</b>
Night mode displays red text on black — preserving your eyes' dark adaptation for stargazing.

<b>Example configurations</b>
Ready-to-import <a href="https://github.com/RealNoitasinagro/epoch/tree/main/example_configs">example configurations</a> are available on GitHub,
including spaceflight tab, world timezone explorer, and config manual.

<b>Privacy first</b>
No ads, notifications, tracking, analytics, or network requests.
All settings stored locally on your device.
No permissions required.
For Local Mean Sidereal Time: optionally grant location access once (coarse, no GPS) or enter longitude manually.

Available in English and German.
